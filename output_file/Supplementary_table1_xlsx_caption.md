## Legend (English, manuscript paste)

**Appendix Table S1. Processed-cohort inventories for GSE155622, GSE141259 and HGSOC.**

Three worksheets, one per adopted LDH-scRNA checkpoint. Counts are from that checkpoint’s `obs.csv` after HVG processing and cell-type coding, not from the unfiltered GEO/EGA objects. Time or phase labels are experimental sampling stages (injury day or treatment-naive vs post-NACT), not dynamical steady states.

**Sheet GSE155622 (mouse DRG, spared-nerve injury).** (A) Overview: accession, tissue, six `condition` stages (Control; SNI 6 h, 24 h, 2 d, 7 d, 14 d), cell-type key `annotation` (nine compartments), 3000-gene panel / latent dim 384, validation recorded as random cell hold-out. (B) Cells per stage. (C) Cells and fractions per type. (D) Stage × type counts (`n_total` is the row sum). Snapshots are unpaired across time.

**Sheet GSE141259 (mouse whole lung, bleomycin).** (A) Overview: seven `stage` days (D0–D28), 15 major metacelltypes, 5000-gene panel / latent dim 512, D28 time-extrapolation hold-out. (B) Cells per day and number of mice (`orig.ident`). (C) Type counts with `formal_label`. (D) Stage × type counts using formal labels. Cross-sectional; PBS/bleomycin mice are not a paired lineage.

**Sheet HGSOC (human NACT-paired).** Restricted to `annotation` ∈ {EOC, Immune, Stromal}. Time axis is `treatment_phase` (treatment-naive, TN; post-NACT, PN), two points only. Clinical stage (IIIC/IVA/IVB) is a conditioner, not time. (A) Overview: 11 patients, 3000-gene panel / latent dim 512, patient hold-out. (B) Cells per phase. (C) Type counts. (D) Per-patient TN/PN counts by type. (E) Sample inventory (patient × phase × sample × anatomical site × clinical stage). Training OT pairing is within `patient_id`; this sheet is an inventory, not the transport benchmark.

## 中文表注

**附表 S1. GSE155622、GSE141259、HGSOC 处理后队列清单（Excel，三张工作表）。**

细胞数来自各采用 checkpoint 的 `obs.csv`，不是原始未过滤矩阵。时间/阶段为实验取样标签，不是稳态。

**GSE155622：** 小鼠 DRG、SNI。A 概览（6 个 condition、9 类 annotation、3000 基因 / 384 维、随机细胞 hold-out）；B 各损伤阶段细胞数；C 各类型计数与比例；D 阶段×类型交叉表。时间点之间无同一细胞配对。

**GSE141259：** 小鼠全肺、博来霉素。A 概览（D0–D28 共 7 天、15 个 metacelltype、5000 基因 / 512 维、D28 时间外推）；B 各天细胞数及小鼠数；C 类型及正式名称；D 阶段×类型（正式标签）。截面设计，非谱系追踪。

**HGSOC：** 仅 EOC / Immune / Stromal。时间轴仅为治疗前 (TN) 与治疗后 (PN) 两档；临床分期不是时间。A 概览（11 名患者、3000 基因 / 512 维、按病人 hold-out）；B 各治疗阶段细胞数；C 类型计数；D 每名患者 TN/PN 分类型计数；E 样本清单（患者×阶段×样本×部位×分期）。本表为清单，不是 hold-out 运输评分。
